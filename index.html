Samuel

Leadership means making room for others to lead.

My work sits at the intersection of youth leadership, facilitation, innovation and community impact. I’m interested in practical ways to help young people find their voice, work together, access opportunities and move from ideas to action.

Through initiatives connected to youth participation, leadership development and innovation, I contribute to spaces where young people can learn, collaborate and build solutions to challenges around them.

This site is a growing record of that work: the projects, tools, lessons and ideas I’m developing along the way.
